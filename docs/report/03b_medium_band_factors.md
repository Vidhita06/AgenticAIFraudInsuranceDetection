## Reading

- Medium-band claims with a concrete factor (simulated data off): fraud rate 4/71 = 5.6%; without: 20/202 = 9.9%. The rate with a factor is **not higher** (95% intervals [2.2%, 13.6%] vs [6.5%, 14.8%]).
- The red flags and the red-flag score are model features, so once a claim's score falls in the medium band they carry little extra information; every medium-band validation claim has at least one red flag and a positive red-flag-score SHAP contribution.
- So the factors are a requirement for an *explainable* flag (an adjuster can check them), not a proven way to find more fraud inside the medium band. With 24 frauds the intervals are wide; the live evaluation reports FRAUD_RISK flags by risk band with their fraud rate, which shows whether the agent's medium-band flags add value.
