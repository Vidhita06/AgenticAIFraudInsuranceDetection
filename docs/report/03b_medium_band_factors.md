## Reading

- Medium-band claims with a factor from the previous list (warning red flag, policy FAIL, similar-claim fraud, strong SHAP driver, conflict; simulated data off): fraud rate 4/71 = 5.6%; without: 20/202 = 9.9%. The rate with a factor is **not higher** (95% intervals [2.2%, 13.6%] vs [6.5%, 14.8%]).
- The red flags and the red-flag score are model features, so once a claim's score falls in the medium band they carry little extra information; every medium-band claim has at least one red flag and a positive red-flag-score SHAP contribution.
- With 24 frauds the intervals are wide; the live evaluation reports FRAUD_RISK flags by risk band with their fraud rate.

## Decision

Because these model-derived factors do not separate fraud inside the medium band, flagging the 71 claims that have one would be cost-negative at the 10:1 cost ratio (~4 frauds for ~67 false flags), so the medium-band flag triggers (`agent.medium_band_concrete_factors`) are now limited to information the model does not see — a policy-condition FAIL (tagged POLICY_CONDITION, not FRAUD_RISK), a claim-record contradiction, or a claim-history contradiction — while red flags, SHAP drivers and similar-claim fraud remain in the rationale as context only. Medium-band validation claims with a current trigger: 0 with simulated data off, 13 with it on.
